THE FAMILY

A family run business. The architect who designed the houses is the one who lives here.

Drawn byINGA

Inga is an architect and designed the project. Ísak and their sons helped build the houses. They still run the place themselves.

OneARCHITECT

All the houses are designed by the same architect, so they share a style and differ in what they give you. Ninety-three square metres each, a fully equipped kitchen, a dining area, a living room, and a private terrace facing the mountains.

What isINCLUDED

Bed linen, towels and the final cleaning. Bathrobes and slippers. Coffee, tea, olive oil, salt and pepper in the kitchen. Underfloor heating, a washing machine and a tumble dryer in every house.

Twelve plates

of what grows where the houses stand

The heath the houses stand on is not empty. Mountain avens and moss campion in the gravel, arctic thyme where it is dry, cotton grass where it is wet, and woolly fringe moss over the lava the whole way to the crater. Twelve of them were painted for this site.

A HOUSE IN A LANDSCAPE BORROWS EVERYTHING FROM IT, so it is worth knowing what is actually out there, at ankle height, between the terrace and the mountain.
